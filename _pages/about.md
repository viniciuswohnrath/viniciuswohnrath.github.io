## About

My research examines the production, circulation, and appropriation of knowledge, pedagogical traditions, and forms of legitimacy in Latin America, particularly in Brazil and Mexico.

In Brazil, I investigated the participation of legal, political, and religious actors in the institutionalization of rights through sociohistorical and prosopographic approaches.

In Mexico, I conduct ethnographic research in Cherán K’eri, Michoacán, focusing on indigenous teacher education and the appropriation of critical pedagogies, particularly Freirean thought.
