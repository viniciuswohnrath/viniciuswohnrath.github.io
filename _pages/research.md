My research examines the social production of authority — how agents and institutions acquire, exercise, and contest the power to define social reality. Empirically, this question has led me to three connected sites of investigation.

---

## Current Projects

### Indigenous Education and Political Formation
Ethnographic research on indigenous teacher formation and the appropriation of Freirean pedagogy in Cherán K'eri, Michoacán. The project examines how critical pedagogy is reconverted into political and pedagogical capital within indigenous community governance structures.

**Funding:** SECIHTI, Mexico.

---

### Religion, Politics, and Public Space
Research on religious institutions, secularism, and symbolic conflict in contemporary Latin America, with focus on the role of Catholic and evangelical actors in educational and constitutional disputes in Brazil.

**Funding:** CNPq, Brazil · in collaboration with UERJ.

---

## Previous Projects

### Sociology of Legal Elites and Doctoral Education in Brazil (2017–2022)
Research on juridical expertise and elite formation in Brazilian law schools. The project examined how doctoral programs in law produce and legitimate legal authority and constitutional interpretation.

**Funding:** FAPESP · UNICAMP · ENS Paris-Saclay.

---

### Catholic Intellectuals, Childhood Rights, and State Formation (2013–2017)
Doctoral research on Catholic intellectuals and constitutional disputes during Brazil's democratic transition, examining the circulation of religious expertise within state institutions after 1988.

**Funding:** FAPESP · CAPES · EHESS Paris.

Prosopography · Socio-historical Analysis · Ethnography · Comparative-Historical Analysis
